Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

RM Creator Tools ("the tool") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the tool accesses YouTube data for the owner's channels only: channel and video metadata, comments on the owner's videos, and YouTube Analytics reports. The tool uses YouTube API Services; by using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How the data is used

  • To upload, schedule and update videos and thumbnails on the owner's channels.
  • To read comments and post replies that the owner has approved.
  • To prepare analytics reports for the owner.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens and data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising. Public comment text may be processed by an AI assistant operated by the owner solely to draft replies for the owner's approval.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored tokens and cached data are deleted by the owner on request or when no longer needed, and in any case within 30 days of revocation.
